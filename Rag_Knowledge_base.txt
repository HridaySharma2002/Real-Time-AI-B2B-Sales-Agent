[SECTION: PRODUCT_OVERVIEW]
Title: Enterprise AI B2B Sales Agent Platform Overview
Category: product_overview
Content:
ApexSales AI is an enterprise real-time AI B2B Sales Agent platform designed to automate inbound lead qualification, real-time voice & chat discovery calls, and instant demo booking. It integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack. Key capabilities include sub-500ms voice conversational latency, dynamic RAG for instant objection handling, and automatic CRM note synchronization.

[SECTION: PRODUCT_FEATURES]
Title: Real-Time Voice and Conversational Latency
Category: product_features
Content:
ApexSales AI achieves ultra-low conversational latency under 500 milliseconds using high-performance streaming STT (Speech-to-Text), streaming local/cloud LLMs (including Qwen and specialized sales models), and neural TTS synthesis. It sounds completely natural, handles turn-taking, and handles user interruptions seamlessly.

Title: Pricing Plans & Enterprise Tiers
Category: pricing
Content:
ApexSales AI offers three tiers:
1. Starter Plan: $499/month - Includes 1 AI sales agent, 1,000 voice minutes, standard CRM sync.
2. Growth Plan: $1,499/month - Includes up to 5 AI sales agents, 5,000 voice minutes, custom RAG knowledge base, multi-language support, HubSpot & Salesforce integrations.
3. Enterprise Custom: Starting at $3,500/month - Unlimited agents, dedicated sub-300ms voice pipelines, on-prem/private VPC Ollama deployment option, SOC2 compliance, custom CRM integrations, and dedicated account manager.
Annual billing includes a 20% discount.

[SECTION: OBJECTION_HANDLING]
Title: Objection: Price is Too High / Budget Constraints
Category: objection_handling
Content:
When a prospect says 'It's too expensive' or 'We don't have budget right now':
1. Acknowledge and reframe to ROI: 'I completely understand budget is top of mind. Most of our clients see a 4x ROI within the first 60 days because the AI qualifies leads 24/7 without hiring additional SDRs.'
2. Offer a proof of concept (POC) or starting pilot on the Starter/Growth plan.
3. Highlight cost comparison: An in-house SDR costs $75k+/year; ApexSales handles 10x the call volume at a fraction of the cost.

[SECTION: OBJECTION_HANDLING]
Title: Objection: We Already Use Another Tool / Competitor Comparison
Category: objection_handling
Content:
When a prospect mentions existing solutions (e.g., standard chatbots, Drift, or traditional dialers):
1. Clarify the differentiator: 'Unlike static chatbots or slow IVRs, ApexSales operates with sub-500ms real-time voice latency and deep RAG context, meaning it can hold fluid phone conversations and answer complex technical sales questions instantly.'
2. Offer side-by-side pilot comparison with zero setup downtime.

Title: Objection: Concerns About Security, Privacy & Data Compliance
Category: objection_handling
Content:
